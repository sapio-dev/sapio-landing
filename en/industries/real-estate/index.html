FAQ for real estate

Do you handle leads from listing sites?

Yes. Once your CRM is connected, we pick them up within minutes and message them on WhatsApp. Otherwise, you forward us the email the listing site sends for each new lead, and we write to them all the same.

Do you respect the rep already assigned to each lead?

Yes. If your CRM says the prospect is Paula’s, we handle them and anything that needs a person goes only to her. If there’s no rep yet, we assign one using your rules: by development, or taking turns among your reps.

What exactly do you do in my CRM?

We read new prospects from it, plus your developments and units with their prices. And we log every step under the rep’s name, with the prospect’s temperature and when to call back.

What if the prospect wants to talk to a person?

We hand them to their rep with the full context: what they’re looking for, their income, their down payment and what they’ve already been told. If nobody picks it up within 30 minutes, we send the rep a reminder by email.

Can it promise discounts?

No. It presents promotions as “up to”, within the cap you have loaded, and the exact discount is for your rep. If someone asks for a price in another currency, it converts it at that day’s rate.
