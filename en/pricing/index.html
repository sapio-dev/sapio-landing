What counts as a unit?

We choose it with you during the analysis. To be billed, it has to pass three rules.

The system counts it on its own

It’s recorded in your calendar, your CRM or your store. Nobody logs it by hand, and nobody argues about it at the end of the month.

The system does it, not a person

If someone on your team or ours finished it by hand, it isn’t a unit.

It depends on us

We charge for what’s in our hands: the viewing we booked, not the sale your rep closes.

FAQ

Why don’t you publish prices?

Because the amount depends on your work and on what it costs you today. We calculate it in the analysis, before you sign anything.

What do I compare it with?

With what you pay for that work today: your own team, a call center, an agency or a lead provider. Not with a software license.

What if a month is slow?

You pay the fixed fee and the units there were. No work, no units to bill.

And if there’s more work than expected?

You pay up to the cap: your bill never goes over it, and the service doesn’t stop.

How do I know how many units you did?

Each one is recorded in your calendar, your CRM or your store, and you see them in your Monday report.

Do you charge for what my team resolves?

No. If it went to your team and a person resolved it, it isn’t our unit.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. You pay month to month and can leave whenever you want.
