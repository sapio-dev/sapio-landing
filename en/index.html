FAQ

More questions
What kind of work can you do?

Any work your team does on a computer and could teach to someone new: answering and booking, quoting, collecting, coordinating, writing or drawing. We look at it with you in the free analysis.

We already tried software and nobody used it. What’s different?

We don’t leave you a tool: we do the work ourselves, every day, and every Monday we tell you what got done.

Does it replace my team?

No. We take the repetitive part and leave your team what needs judgment, with the full context.

Do I need a technical team?

No. We build, connect and look after everything. Your team just tells us how they work.

What if it gets something wrong?

We catch it, fix it and tell you on Monday. What fails on our side isn’t charged.

How much does it cost?

A monthly price for the work we do, with a cap your bill never goes over. We work it out in the analysis, against what that work costs you today with people.
